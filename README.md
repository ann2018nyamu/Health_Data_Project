## Healthcare Landscape Analysis in Kenya
This project investigates the relationship between hospital infrastructure, disease burden, and mortality trends in Kenya between 2010 and 2015.
It highlights disparities across counties, explores data-driven correlations, and offers recommendations for policymakers and public health stakeholders.

## Project Objective
To explore how disparities in hospital availability may contribute to variations in disease burden and mortality across Kenyan counties, 
and to identify high-risk areas that require targeted healthcare investments.

## Key Questions
- How does hospital availability correlate with disease incidence at the county level?
- Can national mortality trends provide useful insights into healthcare challenges even without county-level death data?

## Data Overview

## Source: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS)

## Data Transformation Techniques:
- Standardized column naming
- Data unpivoting for normalization
- Data cleaning (formatting, trimming, capitalization)
- Null/decimal value handling and rounding

## Insights & Recommendations
- Counties with the highest number of hospitals also have high disease burden — suggesting that care may be reactive rather than preventive.
- Communicable diseases remain a significant challenge, highlighting the need for stronger public health interventions.
- Hospital infrastructure continues to grow, but gaps in preventive care persist.
- Spikes in mortality, especially in 2014, underline the need for better emergency response and national disease management.

## Recommendations
- Shift focus to preventive care.
- Target - high-burden counties with tailored health strategies.
- Improve disease surveillance and reporting systems across all regions.
- Scale successful AIDS treatment programs to underserved counties.

## Limitations

- Incomplete or inconsistent data in 2013 and 2014.
- Lack of county-level death statistics limited granular analysis.
- Hospital count used as a proxy for capacity (beds, staff, services not included).
